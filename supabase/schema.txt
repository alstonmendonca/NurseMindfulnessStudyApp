Table: app_usage_sessions

id (int4, PK) → Primary key

participant_number (int4) → Foreign key to participants.participant_number

session_start (timestamptz) → When the app was opened/session started

session_end (timestamptz) → When the app was closed/session ended

duration_minutes (int4) → Duration of the session in minutes

app_version (varchar) → App version for tracking

created_at (timestamptz) → Record creation timestamp
